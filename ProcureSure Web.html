How It Works

From Registration to Payment — Made Simple

Six steps from registering your produce to receiving payment in your bank account.

01

Register Your Produce

Enter your name, email and phone number on the web form. Crop details are a planned later step.

Takes under 2 minutes
02

Get Your Digital Token

A unique token number (e.g., #PS105) and entry QR code are planned for delivery by SMS.

Instant verification code
03

Smart Time Prediction

A planned AI model would calculate your arrival window from weighing throughput, line length and tractor speeds.

Dynamic recalculation
04

Arrive at the Right Time

Drive to the Direct Procurement Centre within your designated 30-minute arrival window to avoid roadside congestion.

Dedicated express gate lane
05

Track Your Produce

The planned portal would show digital scale readings, moisture readings (e.g. 14.2%) and grade receipts on your phone.

Certified electronic receipt
06

Track Your Payment

Planned: payment records sent to the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) for bank settlement alerts.

Direct Benefit Transfer

Impact

Creating a Better Procurement Experience

Four outcomes that matter most to farmers, procurement staff and the state.

Less Unnecessary Waiting

Aims to reduce exhausting multi-day roadside camping outside procurement centres with scheduled arrival slots and notifications.

Better Procurement Transparency

Aims to reduce unofficial middlemen and arbitrary weighment deductions, and keeps moisture test records on file.

Improved Centre Management

Assists procurement officers in regulating tractor yard throughput, avoiding road gridlock and ensuring smooth daily dispatches.

Greater Payment Visibility

Clear tracking milestones from crop weighment entry to bank settlement reduce farmer anxiety and speed up seasonal financial planning.
